How to play

The game has one simple goal:
collect all the coins .

  • Use the LEFT and RIGHT arrow keys to move around.
  • The UP key is to jump.
  • Don't let the lava touch you or you will loose a life!
  • Enemies will also take a life!
  • Land on top of the enemy to destroy them!

The player has 3 lives at the start of each level. Once all lives are lost the game will end.

Have fun jumping around. ^_^
